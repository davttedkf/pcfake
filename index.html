FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is $299 the final price?

$299 is the full price of the FX-300 base configuration in Ceramic White. Sales tax, where applicable, is calculated at checkout. There are no additional fees.

Does it come with Windows installed?

Yes. Every unit ships with Windows 11 Home, fully activated and up to date, with no trial software or pre-installed extras.

Can I upgrade it later?

Yes. The FX-300 uses standard, off-the-shelf parts throughout: additional RAM slots, a free M.2 slot, and a side panel that opens with two thumbscrews. No proprietary components.

When does the first run go on sale?

December 24 at 1:00 PM Eastern Time. Orders placed at launch ship within 24 hours and typically arrive in 5–7 business days. Every unit is inspected and burn-in tested before it leaves our facility.
